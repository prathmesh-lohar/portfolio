PanditPlus – Online Pandit Booking Platform

A comprehensive digital service connecting Yajmans with certified Vedic Pandits for religious ceremonies, featuring distinct user and priest dashboards, custom ritual packages, and real-time appointment booking.

Project Overview

PanditPlus is an innovative online booking platform that bridges the gap between Yajmans (devotees) and qualified, certified Pandits for religious rituals, pujas, havans, and spiritual consultancies.

The platform addresses common pain points in arranging traditional ceremonies by introducing transparency, schedule coordination, and ceremony customization. It features dual role interfaces—a client portal for Yajmans to discover and book pujas, and a dedicated service management portal for Pandits to manage calendars, accept rituals, and review client requests.

Constructed with Django (Python) on the backend and Bootstrap on the frontend, PanditPlus delivers high performance, secure authentication, and database resilience hosted on AWS EC2 with Nginx.

Key Features

  1. 1) Dual Role Interfaces: Personalized dashboards tailored specifically for devotees (Yajmans) and religious service providers (Pandits).
  2. 2) Instant Ceremony Booking: Select dates, preferred languages, and ritual packages with instant scheduling.
  3. 3) Granular Puja Categorization: Browse catalogs for Griha Pravesh, Satyanarayan Katha, Rudra Abhishek, Vastu Shanti, Vivah Sanskar, and more.
  4. 4) Location & Date Matching: Smart recommendations that pair devotees with nearby qualified pandits matching their traditions and calendar.
  5. 5) Lifecycle Booking Management: Real-time tracking of upcoming, ongoing, and completed puja services.
  6. 6) Responsive Mobile-First Design: Clean, high-contrast, fully accessible layout across mobile, tablet, and desktop screens.
